AI in Healthcare

What you will learn: basic AI concepts, examples of AI in healthcare, and why human review matters.

Course introduction

Artificial intelligence is a broad term for computer systems that perform tasks such as finding patterns in information. In healthcare, these tools may support research and professional workflows. They do not replace qualified healthcare professionals.

Quick reflection

When a computer system gives a result, what information would a professional need before deciding whether that result is useful?
